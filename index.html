The Right Material for Industrial Containers Starts with Polypropylene

Polypropylene Containers Built for Industrial Scale

Polypropylene (PP) has become the definitive material choice for high-volume industrial container applications — and the reasons are practical, not theoretical. PP combines a high strength-to-weight ratio, outstanding chemical resistance, full food-contact compliance under FDA 21 CFR regulations, and a reuse lifespan that far outlasts alternative packaging formats. At Elipacko, our full range of polypropylene containers covers every bulk handling scenario in the modern supply chain.

Whether you are moving fresh produce through a cold chain, consolidating dry goods at a distribution center, or transporting manufactured components between production facilities, the right PP container reduces damage, cuts labor time, and lowers your per-trip packaging cost well below that of any single-use format.

Why Polypropylene Outperforms in Bulk Container Applications

PP is a semi-crystalline thermoplastic with a melting point typically above 320°F (160°C), making it stable across the temperature ranges encountered in food processing, cold-storage logistics, and ambient warehouse environments. Its low density — typically 0.895 to 0.92 g/cm³ — means a polypropylene container weighs significantly less than an equivalent-volume metal or rigid alternative, directly reducing freight costs and worker fatigue during manual handling.

Chemical resistance is another defining characteristic. Polypropylene does not absorb moisture, resists dilute acids, alkalis, and most common cleaning agents, and does not harbor the microbial growth that porous materials can sustain over repeated use cycles. For food and pharmaceutical supply chains, that resistance is not a nice-to-have — it is a baseline requirement.

Trip lifespan matters enormously in the economics of bulk packaging. Elipacko PP containers are engineered and tested for 50 or more round trips under normal load conditions. Spread your container purchase cost across 50+ uses and the per-trip expense becomes a fraction of any single-use alternative. That math compounds quickly when you are running containers through hundreds of shipments per year.

The Elipacko PP Container Range

Elipacko supplies a comprehensive lineup of polypropylene containers to match the full spectrum of bulk handling requirements. Every product in the range is available in virgin PP resin or food-grade post-industrial PP, with custom dimensions available for high-volume programs.

PP Gaylord Boxes

Our polypropylene gaylord boxes are designed to match GMA pallet footprints and stack efficiently in standard racking. Unlike single-use corrugated alternatives, PP gaylord boxes retain their structural integrity through multiple wash cycles, resist moisture infiltration at the base and walls, and can be fitted with optional liners for powders, granules, or liquids. Standard capacities range from 1,000 lb to 2,500 lb static load rating.

Reusable PP Bulk Bins

Collapsible and rigid PP bulk bins provide versatile storage and transport for everything from agricultural produce to automotive components. Collapsible models reduce return freight volume by up to 75% when empty, a critical factor for closed-loop supply chains. Rigid bulk bins offer superior stacking strength for high-density warehouse environments.

PP Harvest Lugs and Produce Crates

Our agricultural PP lugs and produce crates are purpose-designed for the demands of fresh produce handling. Smooth-wall interiors prevent bruising, integrated drainage channels manage condensation, and the open-mesh or solid base options allow airflow or liquid management depending on product requirements. All food-contact surfaces meet FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 standards for olefin polymers.
